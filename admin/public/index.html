Check these

Every night the AI reads each new event's title and venue and publishes the ones a family could enjoy, including general events like festivals or planetarium shows. These are the ones it wasn't sure about. Tap Show or Hide, and it goes live at the next nightly run (or press Publish now). To pull anything else off the site, find it under Events and tap Hide.

    Weekend

    This weekend's edition per town, drafted on Thursday night and published on Friday morning at /de/wochenende/<town>/. Swap a pick, drop one, or change its German line; the English and Chinese lines are written again when it publishes. If you do nothing, it goes out as drafted.

    City alerts

    Rare things in a big city that parents should know before they go out: big demos and marches, evacuations, transit strikes. Every night the AI reads the official notices collected by city-anomalies and keeps the ones that could matter to families. Check the line in each language (facts only, no reassurance), fix the day or time if needed, and press Confirm: it shows as "Good to know" on that town's Discover and weekend pages a few minutes later, until its day is over, and a Rednote poster set for it (three cards and a caption, in Chinese) appears in the Rednote panel.

      Rednote

      Picture sets for 小红书, drawn when the weekend edition comes out on Friday (every weekend town), plus explainers like St. Martin. "Save all" puts a set's pictures on your phone in one go: pick Photos, Files or Rednote in the share menu, and the caption is copied too. On a computer it downloads them as one ZIP. Post them in this order with the title and caption. No links or QR codes anywhere: Rednote punishes them. Parents find the site by searching 小鹿出游 and typing a card's code (like K31-2) into the search box.

      Instagram

      This weekend's carousel for Instagram, in English for expat parents: a cover, one card per town (Berlin, Munich, Frankfurt, Hamburg, Cologne, Düsseldorf) and a closing card, drawn when the weekend edition comes out on Friday. "Save all" puts the pictures on your phone in one go and copies the caption; pick Instagram or Photos in the share menu. On a computer it downloads them as one ZIP. Post them in this order with the caption. Links in Instagram captions don't click, so the cards and caption give each town's short address and the profile's bio links the site. Nothing is posted automatically.

      Usage

      What visitors did on the site, added up per week (Monday to Sunday). Only daily totals are kept, nothing about who did it. "Opened inside WeChat" counts each visit that started in WeChat once. Page views are in Cloudflare Web Analytics.

      Inbox

      Tips, reports, suggestions and answers from parents. Approved tips and answers show on the site after the next nightly rebuild; rejected items never count. An answer two parents agree on goes live without you, so only lone or conflicting answers wait here. Taps like “We went” are counted without review.

        Season lists

        When a season's dates come out (Christmas markets in October, Karneval parades in December and January), the AI reads each city's list page and drafts the list below. Untick anything wrong, then approve the town: its list replaces the town's list on the site with the next deploy. Rows link to the page they came from.

        Keywords

        Only for events the AI hasn't sorted yet (for example when no AI key is set): those are published when their title or venue contains one of these keywords. Parts of words count, so “kind” also finds Kinderflohmarkt.

          Events

          Override a single event, or fix its title and address. Every change saves automatically and goes live at the next nightly run, or when you press Publish now.

            Sources

            Cleared sources are in by default. Including any other source puts its event facts on the public site, so only switch one on once you're happy with its rights.

            Edit event

            Leave a field as it is to keep the source's value. Changing the street drops the source's map point, so the event shows at town level until it has one again.
